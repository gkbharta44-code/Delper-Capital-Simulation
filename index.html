Delper capital simulation, running

Runs the summary's annual model forward one year at a time, without stopping, until you pause it. Each step applies the same equations as Sections 9a and 10.

Inputs

Year 1 uses the summary's trading capital of $2.214M and 17.6 turns. Turns from Year 2 are solved so that GMV equals the target share of world GDP in the target year, capped at the summary's 26-turn limit. With the cap on, GMV in any year is limited to the target share of world GDP. Changing an input restarts the run from Year 1. The run stops at Year 15.
